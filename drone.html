Aerial Security

Drone Security
Solutions

Skylink extends protection beyond the walls of your property with intelligent drone surveillance — giving you a complete aerial view of your estate, perimeter, and surroundings in real time. Where traditional cameras have blind spots, drones don't.

Our drone security offering integrates directly with the Skylink platform. Alerts, footage, and deployment commands are all managed from the same interface you already use — no separate app, no added complexity.

In partnership with

Certified Aerial Operations Partner

We proudly collaborate with RPAS Consulting — a trusted leader in remotely piloted aircraft systems, bringing certified aerial intelligence to every Skylink deployment.

Real-Time Aerial Monitoring

Live drone feeds stream directly into your Skylink dashboard, giving you an unobstructed bird's-eye view of your property — day or night, on demand.

Wide Perimeter Coverage

A single drone patrol covers kilometres of perimeter that would require dozens of fixed cameras — eliminating blind spots and reducing infrastructure costs.

Rapid Incident Response

When an alert is triggered, a drone can be deployed within seconds — reaching the scene and transmitting live footage before any ground team arrives.

Scalable & Cost-Effective

Replace or supplement expensive fixed infrastructure with flexible drone patrols — scalable to your property size with no permanent installation required.
